1. What PoolHelp is

PoolHelp is a personal tool that helps you track your pool's water chemistry, log tests and chemical additions, set reminders, and get educational guidance and dosing suggestions. Guidance is based on widely used, established pool-care practices (the TroubleFreePool methodology).

PoolHelp is an informational and organizational tool. It is not a professional pool service, and it does not replace a licensed pool professional, a physical test kit, or the instructions on your chemical products. See the Safety & Disclaimer for important details.

2. Optional account, your data

PoolHelp does not require an account. By default, your data is stored only on your device. You can create an optional account, with an email address and password or with Sign in with Apple, to back up and sync your data across devices. While you are signed in, your pools, water test logs, chemical/dosing logs, and reminders sync to our database (hosted with Supabase), as described in the Privacy Policy. Chat and diagnosis history are never synced and stay on your device.

You are responsible for your device, for keeping your account credentials secure, and for keeping your own backups if you want them. Uninstalling the app or resetting its data will permanently remove the data on that device. Resetting while signed in also removes your synced data. Uninstalling does not remove synced data; to remove it, use Reset all data while signed in, use Delete account on the Account screen, or contact us.

3. Optional AI features

AI features (the assistant, photo diagnosis, and test-strip scanning) are optional. When you use one, the request content (your question or photo, plus a summary of your pool's readings and profile, and a one-line weather summary if the pool has a location) is sent to PoolHelp's AI service, a relay operated by Sabesoft LLC on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards it to Anthropic to generate the response (see the Privacy Policy for details). When the AI service is unavailable, these features run in an on-device demo mode.

  • AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Treat it as a starting point, not a final answer, and verify anything important (see Section 5).
  • We may change, limit, or discontinue AI features, or make them part of a paid tier, at any time.
